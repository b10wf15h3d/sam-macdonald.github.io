// 01 about

Break things. Build people.

By day I work in offensive security — pentesting, red teaming and finding the holes before someone else does. The rest of my time goes into the side that doesn't make it into the report: the people.

I write field guides on burnout and imposter syndrome, run a security social in Tunbridge Wells, and speak at conferences about what happens when we forget to patch ourselves.
